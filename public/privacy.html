HAMIT CONSTRUCTION MANAGEMENT

Privacy policy

Hamit Development Inc. operates Hamit Construction Management, a workspace for construction companies and their teams. Contact us at rafgeneralcontracting@gmail.com about privacy or your data.

Information the app handles

We process your name, email, login credentials, company membership and permissions; project names, addresses and site coordinates; drawings, markups, measurements, reports, photos, signatures, manpower records, RFIs, submittals and attachments you or your team provide. These records may include information about workers, clients and other project participants.

Why we use it

We use this information to authenticate users, control company access, organize projects, synchronize files, prepare reports, send project communications when requested, provide support, prevent abuse and operate the service.

Service providers and optional tools

Supabase provides authentication, database and private file storage. Connecting Google or Microsoft email authorizes the app to send project messages through your mailbox; we store connection details and authorization tokens for that purpose. You may revoke access in your provider account settings. Project addresses may be sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim for geocoding, and coordinates to Open-Meteo for jobsite weather.

If you choose the AI sign-in sheet reader, the selected image is sent to OpenAI to extract names, companies and dates. The entire image may contain signatures or other information even though the reader is instructed to ignore signatures. Use this optional tool only for information you are authorized to share. Review its results before saving.

When store subscriptions are enabled, Apple or Google processes payment details. RevenueCat validates subscription status using a company billing identifier, product identifiers and purchase records. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Sharing

Authorized users in your company can access information according to their assigned permissions. Reports and review links you share can be accessed by their recipients. Exported copies are controlled by their recipients. We also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect the service. We do not sell personal information or use advertising trackers in the app.

Devices and offline files

Camera and photo access is used when you attach jobsite photos. Downloaded drawings are stored on the device for offline access. Signing out removes the app’s offline drawing cache; files you exported or saved to other applications remain under your control.

Retention and deletion

Account information and company records remain while needed to provide the service. You can initiate account deletion in Home → Account & subscription → Delete account, or through our web deletion page. Deletion requests are recorded for processing; submitting one does not mean your account has already been deleted.

Deletion removes the login, profile and connected mailbox credentials. Shared construction records may remain with the company that controls them, including records needed for its projects or legal obligations. Company owners can also request workspace deletion. We may contact you to verify authority, arrange an ownership transfer, or explain specific legally required retention. Existing backups and provider logs can persist until their retention periods expire. We do not promise immediate removal from backup copies or third-party exports.

Your choices

You can edit project information you have permission to change, remove offline copies, disconnect mailbox access, and request access, correction or deletion through support. The service is intended for business users and is not directed to children.
