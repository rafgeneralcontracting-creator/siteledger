Hamit Construction Management is operated by Hamit Development Inc. Use the app only for company and project information you are authorized to manage. Keep your account secure and review team permissions and any reports, AI results or measurements before relying on them.

Company subscriptions

Subscriptions cover one company workspace and the features and allowances displayed for the selected plan. Eligible new subscribers may receive 3 days free, followed by an automatically renewing monthly subscription at the store-displayed price unless cancelled. The trial begins after you confirm the subscription with the payment provider, not when you create an account.

The app must show the total renewal price, billing period and applicable trial terms before confirmation. Your Apple or Google subscription settings control cancellation and refunds for purchases through those stores. Cancel before the trial ends; Apple advises cancelling a free trial at least 24 hours before its end. Deleting the app does not cancel payment.

Company owners manage the subscription for their workspace. Team members do not each need a separate purchase. We do not automatically charge usage overages. Upgrades or other charges require separate consent.

Your company controls its construction records and is responsible for lawful collection and sharing. Keep independent copies of critical documents. The app assists coordination and does not replace professional design, engineering, inspection, contractual or safety decisions.
