Support

For help signing in, company access, drawings, reports or subscriptions, email rafgeneralcontracting@gmail.com.

Include your company name, device, app version, what you expected and what happened. Never send passwords, payment card details or confidential client drawings unless specifically needed through an agreed secure method.

Subscriptions

The planned offer is a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers, followed by the selected monthly company subscription unless cancelled. The store confirmation shows the actual price, eligibility and billing date. Creating an account alone does not start a trial.

Cancel Apple subscriptions in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Cancel Google Play subscriptions in Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. A deletion request is separate from store cancellation.

Account deletion

You can request deletion online without reinstalling the app. If you cannot sign in, contact support from your account email for identity verification.
