## Full description for both stores

Hamit Construction Management helps construction teams keep jobsite information organized and accessible from the field and office.

Create a company workspace, organize projects and assign your team. Record daily work, manpower, deliveries and issues. Add progress photos and prepare client-ready PDF reports.

Open project drawings, scroll through sheets, zoom into details and add markups. Download selected drawings for offline access. Keep RFIs and submittals connected to the project so your team can track questions and review responses.

Core tools include:
- Company workspaces and team permissions
- Project drawings, markups and measurements
- Daily reports, manpower and progress photos
- Daily and weekly PDF report sharing
- RFIs and submittal tracking

An account and internet connection are required for sign-in and cloud updates. Offline access is limited to drawings you have downloaded; this is not a claim that every feature works offline.
